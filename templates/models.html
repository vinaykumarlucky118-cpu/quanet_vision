AI Architecture & Dynamic Model Benchmarks

Detailed mathematical and architectural documentation of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), Multimodal Data Fusion, and authentic empirical benchmark evaluations.

1. CNN Satellite Model

Spatial Feature Extractor

Extracts spectral reflectance patterns, algal blooms, and suspended particulate turbidity from satellite imagery tiles ($128\times 128\times 3$).

Layers: Conv2D(16) → BatchNorm → MaxPool → Conv2D(32) → Conv2D(64) → Dense Embedding (32D)

2. LSTM Time-Series Model

Temporal Dynamics

Analyzes multi-parameter continuous sequence sliding windows to capture rate of change and diurnal temperature/DO cycles.

Layers: Input(Seq x 6) → LSTM(64, 2 layers) → Dropout(0.2) → Dense(32) → Softmax(3)

3. Multimodal Data Fusion

Late Multimodal Layer

Merges spatial computer vision embeddings with temporal sensor representation vectors to compute robust joint classifications.

Equation: $P_{\text{fused}} = \alpha \cdot P_{\text{LSTM}} + (1-\alpha) \cdot P_{\text{CNN}}$ ($\alpha = 0.60$)
