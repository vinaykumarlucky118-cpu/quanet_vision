The Challenge

Why Water Quality Monitoring Matters

Freshwater ecosystems and reservoirs face increasing pressure from industrial runoff, agricultural effluent, and urban discharge.

Limitations of Traditional Monitoring

  • Time-Consuming & Slow: Manual water sample collection and laboratory titration take days to deliver results.
  • High Expense & Infrastructure: Recurring physical laboratory logistics and manual staffing costs.
  • Difficult to Scale: Remote and expansive lakes cannot be physically sampled across every coordinate frequently.
  • Delayed Pollution Detection: Contamination spikes are discovered only after ecological damage or public health risks have emerged.

The QuaNet Vision Approach

QuaNet Vision proposes an intelligent hybrid deep learning framework. By pairing local in-situ IoT telemetry (capturing instantaneous chemistry) with satellite remote sensing (capturing wide-area spectral dispersion), the system eliminates blind spots and delivers automated early warnings.

Architecture

Our Intelligent Solution

A modular 4-pillar pipeline transforming raw environmental telemetry into actionable water conservation insights.

1. IoT Monitoring

Collects key multi-parameter indicators including pH, Temp, Turbidity, Dissolved Oxygen, TDS, and Conductivity.

2. Satellite Observation

Analyzes optical satellite imagery across water bodies to capture algal blooms, surface sediment plumes, and turbidity gradients.

3. Deep Learning Fusion

CNN extracts visual textures while LSTM learns time-series parameter trajectory, fusing spatial and temporal feature embeddings.

4. Smart Prediction

Generates instant water quality status (GOOD, MODERATE, POOR), confidence estimates, automated alerts, and scientific advisories.
